# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Support all 17 wallpaper groups by IT number or name, with SO(3) and
  optional independent time reversal.
- Accept the full standard Bilbao Wyckoff-position catalogue, including
  conventional-cell multiplicities and general positions.
- Treat reflections and glides as antiunitary under crystalline equivalence,
  retaining the exact translation in each glide square.
- Add all-group anomaly recipes, spin-flux reductions, and full F/R/U/eta
  evaluations for the two additional product manifolds.
- Solve large cyclic 2-power fractionalization sectors using exact quadratic
  constraints, preserving higher-order and odd-torsion classes.
- Preserve all 276 v0.1.0 paper-example outputs and legacy eta descriptors.
- Add an all-wallpaper example runner, 884 recorded example classifications,
  and reproducible mathematical and packaging checks.
